# greenbea false-`Infeasible` analysis

Problem: NETLIB `greenbea` (2392 rows x 5405 cols, 30,877 nnz; MPS at `.netlib_cache/mps/greenbea.mps`).
Benchmark record (`scripts/benchmark_results.json`): ours **`infeasible`** in 1.75-1.92 s; HiGHS `kOptimal` at **-72555248.12984587** in 0.21-0.22 s.
Re-measured this session (`--only greenbea`): ours `infeasible` 1.73 s; HiGHS `kOptimal` 0.21 s. Direct in-process reps: ours 1.45-1.82 s, always `infeasible`, always at the same iteration with bit-identical numbers (3 runs).

All numbers below were measured in this environment (HiGHS 1.15.1 via `highspy`, this crate at HEAD `ff4dca7`), not estimated. Throwaway, env-gated instrumentation was added locally to `src/simplex.rs` / `src/simplex/extended_dual.rs` (a presolved-`StdForm` dump, extra context at the two `Infeasible` sites, per-check dual-feasibility and drift traces, and three experiment switches), then reverted with `git checkout` and the HEAD wheel reinstalled; nothing was committed. The same-structure sibling `greenbeb` (5405 x 2392, different data) solves correctly and is used as a control throughout.

## Summary

The `Infeasible` is produced by the simplex engine, not presolve: presolve reports `infeasible=false`, and HiGHS solves the exact presolved `StdForm` we hand to the simplex to **Optimal** (both with and without its own presolve). The verdict comes from `extended_dual::solve_lp_dual_extended`'s BFRT-exhausted branch (`src/simplex/extended_dual.rs:2365-2382`) at iteration 4715: the leaving row's deviation is `w_r = 3.0e-7 + 1.0*M` and the single eligible candidate's flip capacity is `0 + 1.0*M`. `Affine1::cmp_lex` (`:676-690`) finds the `M`-slopes tied and decides "still short" on the base channel because `3.0e-7 > 1e-9` -- but a fresh factorization gives the row's base term as **exactly 0**: the 3.0e-7 is Forrest-Tomlin drift (47 updates since the last refactor, on a problem where the drift tolerance had already been escalated to its 1e-4 cap). The candidate covers the deviation exactly; the walk should have stopped on it and pivoted. Instead the branch reports a mathematically-certain `Infeasible` without ever re-checking on a fresh factorization, and the whole `base`-channel tolerance scheme of this module (1e-9 relative-to-the-deviation, i.e. 1e-9 absolute; `noise_feasible` at `PRIMAL_FEAS_TOL * max(|b[r]|, 1)` = 1e-7 absolute because **every RHS in greenbea is 0**) is meaningless on a problem whose basic values reach 2e6-3.3e8.

Classification: **correctness bug -- numerical feasibility-tolerance issue** (an absolute base-channel tolerance far below the drift the module itself tolerates, plus an `Infeasible` conclusion drawn from an updated, unverified factorization). Not presolve, not ill-conditioning of the basis (refactorizations all come from the drift check; DSE weights 99.8% within 1%; dual feasibility is maintained throughout), not degeneracy in the baseline run (3 degenerate pivots of 4716).

Fixing the verdict alone is **not** enough to get the right answer, and that matters for the loop's next step. Two experiments that patch the verdict (a classical-style `reach_tol` in the BFRT walk; a HiGHS-style "refactorize and retry before declaring infeasible") each expose a further, previously invisible defect, both measured below:

* **(B) cost perturbation is never removed**: with the verdict patched, the extended path certifies `Optimal` at **-72462440.34** (0.128% above HiGHS). HiGHS run on the *perturbed* presolved LP reaches a point whose true-cost objective is -72124818.089 (std-form terms) -- our polish end value is -72124818.086. The answer is exactly the optimum of the perturbed problem: max perturbation 3.2e-4 x optimal components of 3.3e8 = ~1e5 of objective.
* **(C) the classical `BIG_M = 1e7` fallback silently truncates**: greenbea's true optimum has two components at **3.3e8 > BIG_M**; whenever the extended loop gives up (`None`), the fallback reports `optimal` at -72465273.02. The sweep shows `dfl001` hits the same fallback (`optimal` 11259043.80 vs HiGHS 11266396.05, *below* the true minimum, i.e. not a feasible point), hidden today by the 60 s benchmark timeout.
* **(D) the extended loop's stall detector is blind to noise pivoting**: with the verify-and-retry patch, `infeasible_rows` stays at 248 and `remaining_m_side` at 1006 from iteration ~8000 to `MAX_ITERS = 20000` with `stall_count = 0`, then falls to (C).

```

```

Reading: the basic structural `4236` (`[0, inf)`, `M`-flagged) sits at `x = -M - 3e-7`, violating its lower bound by `w_r = M + 3e-7`. The one eligible column `4262` is nonbasic at its `+M` side with tableau entry `-1`; flipping it to `0` moves the row by exactly `M` (`width_affine`, `:1378-1393`, returns `(0, 1)` for every `M`-flagged column). The true remainder is `(0, 0)`; the maintained one is `(3.0e-7, -1.5e-15)`. `Affine1::cmp_lex` (`:676-690`): slope difference 1.5e-15 is under `REL_TOL * max(|slopes|, 1) = 1e-9`, so the comparison falls to the base terms, where `3.0e-7 > 1e-9 * max(3.0e-7, 0, 1) = 1e-9` returns `Greater`, the walk runs off the end, `k_star` is `None`, and `:2382` returns `Infeasible`. Note the base tolerance is relative to the *deviation being compared* (`base_scale = max(|self.base|, |other.base|, 1.0)`), which for a remainder of 3e-7 is simply 1e-9 absolute -- it is not related to the magnitude of the numbers the base channel actually carries.

Both this site and the `eligible_empty` site (`:2334`) conclude from the *maintained* `x_B(M)`. The `eligible_empty` site at least has the `noise_feasible` rescue (`:2301`), but that rescue is (a) only applied when `w_r.slope == 0` and (b) scaled by `|b[r]|`, which is 0 on every row of greenbea (HiGHS log: `RHS [0e+00, 0e+00]`), so it floors at 1e-7 absolute. The `bfrt_exhausted` site has no rescue at all.

### What is *not* wrong (measured)

* **Dual feasibility is maintained.** A per-check comparison of the maintained `d` against a fresh BTRAN recompute, restricted to non-fixed nonbasic columns, gives max relative residual 2.9e-7 over 942 checks; the fresh `d` has 0 violations `> 1e-6` at every one of the 50 refactor resyncs; the maintained `d` shows at most 1.1e-5 (3-4 columns, well inside the perturbation). The BFRT-tolerance experiment run: 0 violations in 1319 checks.
  Caveat for future readers: the pre-existing `ENOMOTO_DEBUG_EXT_DUAL_CHECK` (`:2022-2040`) and the `D_DRIFT_TOL` residual (`:3011-3017`) include **fixed columns** (`lb == ub`; here the 1549 equality-row slacks, which PRICE deliberately skips at `:2240`, so their maintained `d` is legitimately stale and a fixed column has no sign requirement). They therefore report a spurious "dual violation 21.8 at iteration 470 on `j=5526`" (slack of equality row 881) and a 12-54% "drift" that is entirely stale fixed-column entries. Both diagnostics need a `std.lb[j] != std.ub[j]` filter before they are trusted.
* **The basis is not ill-conditioned in the sense the profiler tracks**: `verify=0 try_update=0 illcond=0`, DSE 99.8% within 1%, no `try_update` rejection in 4716 pivots.
* **Not degenerate/cycling in the baseline**: 3 degenerate pivots, `bland_mode=false`, `stall_count=0`, `avg_harris_window_size=1.008`.
* **Not presolve** (above).

## Root cause classification

**Correctness bug -- numerical feasibility-tolerance issue in `extended_dual`'s finite ("base") channel, turned into a definitive verdict without verification.** Specifically:

1. `Affine1::cmp_lex` (`src/simplex/extended_dual.rs:676-690`) decides base-channel ties at `1e-9 * max(|a|, |b|, 1)` where `a`, `b` are the *deviations* being compared -- for a small remainder that is 1e-9 absolute, unrelated to the 1e5-1e8 magnitudes the channel carries. It is used both by the feasibility predicate `row_deviation` (`:964-996`, comparisons against `Affine1::ZERO` at `:982-988`) and by the BFRT coverage test (`:2358`).
2. The only noise rescue, `noise_feasible` at the `eligible_empty` site (`:2301`), is gated on `slope == 0` and scaled by `|b[r]|`, which is 0 on all 2056 rows here (1e-7 absolute). The `bfrt_exhausted` site (`:2365-2382`) has no rescue.
3. Both sites conclude from the incrementally maintained `x_B(M)` even though the module's own drift check permits up to `XB_DRIFT_TOL_MAX = 1e-4` of absolute base-channel drift (`:488-511`), and on this problem the residual is 1e-7..5e-3 between refactorizations. The fresh value at the failing row is exactly 0.
4. The classical path already handles this class with a magnitude-scaled `reach_tol` (`src/simplex.rs:4075-4114`, motivated there by `maros` landing 1.04e-7 short of an exact target) and a `noise_feasible` rescue (`src/simplex.rs:3990`); the extended path's analogues never got the scaling.

Downstream defects that any fix will expose (measured above, not hypothesized): (B) no perturbation removal before `Optimal` (92808 objective error at greenbea's scale); (C) `BIG_M = 1e7` fallback truncation reported as `optimal` (greenbea, dfl001); (D) noise-pivot stall invisible to `stall_count`.

## Suggested direction (no changes made)

In priority order; (1)+(2) remove the false verdict, (3)+(4) are required to reach the *right* answer, (5) is a safety net that also covers dfl001.

1. **Never declare `Infeasible` from an updated factorization.** At `:2334` (`eligible_empty`, after the `noise_feasible` test fails) and `:2382` (`bfrt_exhausted`): if `lu.update_count() > 0`, refactorize and fully resync (`x_B(M)`, `InfeasibleRows`, `d`, DSE -- the existing block at `:3032-3058` can be factored into a helper) and `continue`; only a verdict reached with a fresh factorization is reported. This is HiGHS's `kRebuildReasonPossiblyDualUnbounded` discipline (rebuild, then re-check). Measured: rescues both greenbea verdicts (4715, 7734) at the cost of one extra refactor each (~6 ms). Risk: none to correctness (a fresh check can only be stricter); it does not by itself prevent the stall in (4).

2. **Make the base-channel tolerances magnitude-aware, matching the classical path.** (a) In `row_deviation` (`:964-996`), replace the `cmp_lex`-vs-`ZERO` test on the base term with `PRIMAL_FEAS_TOL * max(|x_b_base[i]|, |bound|, 1)` exactly as `row_deviation_plain` (`:1031`) does, keeping the slope test as is. (b) In the BFRT walk (`:2358`), when the slopes tie within `cmp_lex`'s floor, accept a remainder whose base is within `PRIMAL_FEAS_TOL * max(|w_r.base|, |x_b_base[r]|, 1)` (the classical `reach_tol`, `src/simplex.rs:4085`) as "reached" and pivot on that candidate. (c) Scale the `noise_feasible` bar at `:2301` by the row's basic value magnitude, not `|b[r]|`. Expected effect: the 455 "zero-slope, base-decided" and most of the 51 "M-slope, base-decided" verdicts on greenbea become tolerance-consistent, and the noise pivots behind (4) stop being generated. Risk: the `PRIMAL_FEAS_TOL` docs warn that relaxing `chuzr` by row magnitude once degraded objective accuracy on well-behaved instances -- gate on the full 93-problem sweep (`base_decided_*` counters above give the exposure per problem). Note that on greenbea the noise at the failing rows (3e-7, 1.7e-6, 4.2e-7 on 259) exceeds the 1e-7 floor, so (2) without (1) is not sufficient (experiment A + no-perturbation).

3. **Remove the cost perturbation before certifying `Optimal`.** After `polish_with_true_bounds` converges (`:3240` onward; called from `finish` at `:3202`) -- and in the classical `solve_lp_dual_on` likewise -- restore `std.c`, recompute `d`, and if any non-fixed nonbasic column is dual infeasible beyond `DUAL_FEAS_TOL`, run a primal-simplex cleanup (or re-enter the dual loop with the true costs from the current basis). HiGHS does exactly this at the end of dual phase 2. Measured need: 92808 objective error on greenbea with perturbations of at most 3.2e-4. Alternative with less machinery: scale the perturbation by the problem's bound/RHS magnitude so that `max_pert * max|x|` stays below the objective tolerance -- but greenbea's `max|x| = 3.3e8` against costs of 6e-2..1e2 leaves almost no room, so removal is the robust option.

4. **Detect the noise-pivot stall.** `stall_count` stayed 0 while `infeasible_rows` (248) and `remaining_m_side` (1006) did not change for 12,000 iterations. Whatever `stall_count` measures, it should also latch on "no change in `remaining_m_side` and no net decrease of the summed base-channel infeasibility over N iterations", and the response should be a refactor + tolerance-consistent re-scan (with (2) in place these rows should not be selected at all), not `bland_mode` alone. Until then, a `None` from `MAX_ITERS` lands in (5).
